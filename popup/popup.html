How it works. This extension only fills form fields on pages you already opened in your own logged-in session. It never logs in, never solves CAPTCHAs, never submits forms and never touches payment details. Keep it that way: IRCTC blocks accounts that use automated booking.

Tatkal opens at 10:00 IST (AC) and 11:00 IST (non-AC), one day before the journey date.
